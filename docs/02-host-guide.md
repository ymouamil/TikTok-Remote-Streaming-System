# Remote Host Guide

[← Back to Home](../README.md)

---

## Who This Is For

This guide is for the remote host.

The remote host is the person appearing on the TikTok livestream from another location or country.

---

## What You Need

- Discord account
- Stable internet
- Microphone
- Headphones
- Screen or camera to share

Download Discord:

[Download Discord](https://discord.com/download)

---

## Before The Stream

1. Open Discord.
2. Join the server or call provided by the operator.
3. Wear headphones to prevent echo.
4. Close private windows or personal information.
5. Make sure your microphone works.
6. Wait for the operator.

---

## How To Share Your Screen

1. Join the Discord call.
2. Click **Share Screen**.
3. Select the screen or application you want to show.
4. Confirm sharing.
5. Ask the operator if they can see it.

![Share Screen](../images/share-screen.png)

---

## During The Stream

You should:

- Speak clearly.
- Watch the shared chat window.
- Respond verbally to viewer comments.
- Avoid showing private information.
- Tell the operator immediately if video or audio stops.

---

## How To Read TikTok Chat

You do not need to log into TikTok.

The operator will share the TikTok chat window back to you through Discord.

You read the comments and respond verbally during the livestream.

Example:

```text
Viewer: Where are you from?

Host: I'm from Brazil. Thanks for joining!
```

---

## Host Checklist

- [ ] Joined Discord call
- [ ] Headphones on
- [ ] Microphone works
- [ ] Screen share works
- [ ] Can see chat shared by operator
- [ ] Ready to respond verbally
