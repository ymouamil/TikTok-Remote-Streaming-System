# Video Script 2: Host Setup

[← Back to Home](../../README.md)

---

## Goal

Show remote hosts exactly what to do.

---

## Script

First, open Discord.

Join the voice channel or call provided by the operator.

Put on headphones to avoid echo.

Click the Share Screen button.

Choose the screen or application you want to show.

Ask the operator to confirm they can see your screen.

During the stream, watch the chat window the operator shares back to you through Discord.

When viewers ask questions, respond verbally on stream.
