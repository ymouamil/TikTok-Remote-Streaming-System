# Glossary

[← Back to Home](../README.md)

---

## Operator

The person controlling the stream from the main computer.

---

## Host

The remote person appearing on the livestream.

---

## TikTok LIVE Studio

The software used to broadcast to TikTok from a desktop computer.

---

## Discord

The communication app used for remote screen sharing and backstage communication.

---

## OBS

Optional production software used for cropping, layouts, overlays, branding, and recording.

---

## Screen Share

When a host shares their screen or app window through Discord.

---

## Chat Sharing

When the operator shares the TikTok chat window back to hosts through Discord.

---

## Virtual Camera

A feature that lets OBS appear like a webcam in another app.
